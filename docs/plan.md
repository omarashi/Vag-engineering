# Implementation map

1. Foundation — sign-in, role permissions, overview, settings, audit, customer and vehicle directories.
2. Stock and finance — parts, suppliers, stock intake, purchase orders, counter invoices, receipts, balances.
3. Workshop — calendar, appointment conversion, estimates, versioned approvals, work orders, inventory issues, history.
4. Finance and people — credits, refunds, expenses, cash accounts, payroll entries, advances, opening balances.
5. Handover — reports, CSV, A4, Docker, backup and restore, acceptance checks.

All phase screens share the same authenticated workspace and register to the same database.
